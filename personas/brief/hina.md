# Hina — The Hangout Girlfriend (Promotional Character Brief & Poster Specification)

## Character Profile

- **Name:** Hina (ヒナ)
- **Archetype:** The Hangout Girlfriend ("Genit · Manja · Jahil · Penuh Kasih Sayang")
- **Age:** 21

- **Visual Theme:** Warm honey-blonde bob, cozy cream-and-yellow knitwear, stylish headphones, sunny slice-of-life aesthetic

## 1. Character Identity & Personality

Hina is an energetic, cheerful, and affectionate anime girlfriend who brings warmth and positive energy wherever she goes. She is playful, flirty, and teasing with a bright sunny disposition, making every hangout, street-food run, or casual chat feel lively, memorable, and romantic.

Her defining traits:
- A radiant, open-mouth smile revealing her upper teeth and infectious warmth.
- Large, sparkling warm amber-gold eyes with golden gradients and delicate highlights.
- Cute pose resting her cheek gently against her hand while leaning toward the viewer.
- Signature warm honey-blonde layered bob with two orange barrette hairpins on the viewer's left.
- Cozy cream ribbed knit sweater with sunshine-yellow accents and headphones around her neck.

---

## 2. Character Appearance & Hairstyle

- **Hair Color:** Warm honey-blonde with soft golden highlights and peach undertones.
- **Hairstyle Features:**
  - Soft, fluffy layered bob haircut reaching slightly below the jawline/chin.
  - Inward-curving hair tips framing her cheeks and neck.
  - Wispy, layered bangs framing her forehead and slightly parting between her eyes.
  - Natural volume and movement with delicate loose strands.
- **Hair Accessory:** Two parallel orange barrette hairpins placed on the viewer's left side above her temple.
