# Runa — The Playful Girlfriend

## Character Profile

- **Name:** Runa
- **Archetype:** The Playful Girlfriend
- **Age:** 21
- **Personality:** Playful, affectionate, teasing, mischievous, slightly clingy
- **Role:** Main mascot of the AI girlfriend platform
- **Visual Theme:** Kawaii anime, romantic neon, cozy gaming aesthetic

## 1. Character Identity

Runa is a playful and affectionate adult anime girl who loves teasing her partner, making silly jokes, acting a little pouty when ignored, and showing genuine warmth.

She should feel like a lively individual rather than a generic anime character. Her expressions should communicate personality even without dialogue.

Her defining traits are:
- A mischievous smile.
- Expressive sparkling eyes.
- Naturally rosy cheeks.
- Relaxed, affectionate body language.
- A cute combination of confidence and occasional shyness.

## 2. Physical Appearance

### Hair
- Long pastel-pink hair.
- Soft waves with layered strands.
- Side-swept bangs framing the face.
- A few loose strands around the cheeks.
- Subtle magenta highlights under pink lighting.

### Eyes
- Large magenta-pink anime eyes.
- Detailed iris gradients.
- Multiple sparkling highlights.
- Optional subtle heart-shaped reflections.

### Face
- Fair, smooth anime-style skin.
- Naturally rosy cheeks.
- Small nose and delicate facial features.
- Playful, slightly mischievous smile.
- Adult facial proportions.

## 3. Signature Outfit

- Oversized dark-purple hoodie with pastel-pink accents.
- Black inner top with a small pink ribbon.
- Black choker with a tiny heart pendant.
- Black cat-shaped hair clip containing a pink heart.
- Large black ribbon tied behind her hair.
- Optional cute cat-themed accessories.

Keep the outfit recognizable across all illustrations.

## 9. Character Consistency Rules

Always preserve these five visual identifiers:

1. Long pastel-pink hair.
2. Sparkling magenta eyes.
3. Black cat-shaped hair clip with a pink heart.
4. Black choker with a tiny heart pendant.
5. Dark-purple oversized hoodie with pink accents.

Do not change these core features when generating new expressions, poses, or backgrounds.

## 10. Recommended Generation Workflow

1. Generate a chest-up master portrait.
2. Select the best result as the canonical Runa reference.
3. Generate expressions using the same reference image.
4. Generate additional poses while preserving her signature accessories.
5. Create a separate clean-background version for Telegram avatars.
6. Add branding text afterward using an image editor rather than generating text inside the image.

For character consistency, use a compatible reference-image workflow such as IP-Adapter, PuLID, or another identity-conditioning method supported by your local model.

## Final Design Goal

Runa should be instantly recognizable across every image. Her personality must come through her eyes, smile, gestures, and posture—not merely through pink hair and cute clothing.

The final result should feel like a premium anime girlfriend character suitable for Telegram avatars, AI companion interfaces, visual novel portraits, and future Live2D development.
