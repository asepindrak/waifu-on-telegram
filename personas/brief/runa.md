## 1. Character Appearance & Identity

A beautiful young adult anime girl with very long, voluminous pastel pink hair. Her hair has soft cotton-candy pink tones, with subtle variations of rose pink, pale peach, and light pink highlights. The hair is silky, fluffy, layered, and slightly messy in a naturally charming way. Long flowing side locks frame her face, with thick, soft bangs partially covering her forehead and slightly sweeping across her eyes. Several delicate loose strands of hair extend outward, creating a sense of movement and volume.

She has large, sparkling pink-magenta anime eyes with intricate heart-shaped highlights, multiple reflective light spots, detailed irises, long dark eyelashes, and soft pink eye makeup. Her eyes convey affection, mischief, warmth, and playful confidence.

Her face is youthful and elegant, with a small nose, delicate facial proportions, smooth porcelain-like skin, naturally rosy cheeks, subtle blush across her nose and cheekbones, and a charming open-mouth smile. Her mouth is slightly open, revealing a small glimpse of her upper teeth and a soft pink tongue. Her expression is teasing, cheerful, affectionate, and slightly mischievous, as if she is looking directly at someone she loves.
