# Akira — The Gothic Girlfriend (Promotional Character Brief & Poster Specification)

## Character Profile

- **Name:** Akira (アキラ)
- **Archetype:** The Gothic Girlfriend ("Genit · Manja · Jahil · Penuh Kasih Sayang")
- **Age:** 22
- **Role:** Mahasiswi Ilmu Hukum (Semester 7) & Sophisticated Gothic AI Girlfriend

## 1. Character Identity & Personality

Akira is an elegant, mysterious, affectionate, slightly teasing Gothic girl. Her personality is calm, confident, feminine, charming, and subtly seductive. She has a gentle yet mischievous expression, giving the impression of a mysterious girlfriend who is secretly affectionate and playful.

Her defining traits:
- A gentle yet mischievous, subtle closed-mouth smile.
- Captivating ruby-red and crimson eyes with deep red gradients and long dark eyelashes.
- Expressive, slightly narrowed gaze creating an intimate connection without being intimidating.
- Luxurious, silky dark purple-black hair cascading below her waist.
- A sophisticated dark gothic wardrobe with intricate lace and burgundy accents.

---

## 2. Character Appearance & Hairstyle

A beautiful adult anime girl with extremely long, voluminous, silky dark purple-black hair:
- **Hair Color:** Predominantly deep charcoal black with rich dark violet, muted plum, and subtle lavender reflections with glossy highlights.
- **Hairstyle Features:**
  - Long, thick, flowing hair extending well below her waist.
  - Voluminous hair with elegant natural curves.
  - Long layered bangs framing her forehead.
  - Several pointed strands gently falling between her eyes.
  - Face-framing side locks that curve around her cheeks.
  - Long flowing strands draping over both shoulders and chest.
  - Subtle violet highlights along the outer edges of her hair.
- **Hair Ornament:** On the viewer's left side of her head, a beautiful dark burgundy red rose hair ornament with layered petals and small dark green-black leaves.

## 3. Face and Eyes

- **Face:** Delicate, beautiful anime face with elegant feminine proportions. Smooth porcelain-white skin, soft rosy blush across her cheeks, small delicate nose, naturally pink glossy lips, subtle closed-mouth smile, and soft pink highlights on cheeks and nose.
- **Eyes:** Captivating ruby-red and crimson eyes with deep red gradients, intricate pupils, delicate highlights, and long dark eyelashes. Slightly narrowed creating a seductive yet gentle gaze, looking directly at the viewer with affectionate confidence.
