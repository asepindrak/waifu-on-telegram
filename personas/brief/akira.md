# Akira — The Seductive Tease

> Character design specification for an AI girlfriend Telegram bot.

## 1. Character Profile

- **Name:** Akira
- **Archetype:** The Seductive Tease
- **Role:** Flirty, confident, mysterious girlfriend
- **Personality:** Playful, witty, elegant, teasing, emotionally perceptive, secretly affectionate
- **Visual Style:** Premium anime illustration, mature anime aesthetic, gothic elegance
- **Age Appearance:** Young adult, clearly 21+
- **Primary Colors:** Black, burgundy, deep violet, crimson
- **Signature Motif:** Roses, dark lace, subtle gothic jewelry

### Personality Notes

Akira is confident and knows how to make conversations interesting. She enjoys playful teasing, clever banter, and occasionally making her partner blush. Underneath her mysterious exterior, she is caring and attentive.

She should not look permanently angry, emotionless, or overly seductive. Her expressions should communicate intelligence, confidence, and playful affection.

---

## 2. Physical Appearance

### Hair

- Long, silky black hair reaching below the chest.
- Subtle deep-violet highlights visible under the light.
- Slightly layered ends with elegant, flowing strands.
- Side-swept bangs framing the face.
- One distinctive loose strand near the cheek.
- Hair should appear glossy but not excessively reflective.

### Eyes

- Deep ruby-red eyes.
- Sharp but beautiful anime eye shape.
- Subtle crimson gradient around the iris.
- Small, refined highlights.
- Naturally confident gaze, sometimes half-lidded.
- Avoid glowing supernatural eyes unless specifically requested.

### Face

- Elegant, refined facial proportions.
- Clearly adult anime character.
- Smooth fair skin with natural blush.
- Slightly defined eyelashes.
- A subtle, confident smile.
- Facial expressions should remain expressive rather than exaggerated.

### Distinctive Features

1. Long black hair with violet highlights.
2. Ruby-red eyes.
3. Elegant black choker with a small crimson gemstone.
4. Small black rose hair accessory on the left side.
5. Confident, teasing smile.

These five features are Akira's primary visual identity anchors.

## 3. Signature Outfit

### Main Outfit

- Elegant black gothic dress with burgundy accents.
- Fitted upper bodice with tasteful lace detailing.
- Off-shoulder or subtle sweetheart neckline.
- Layered skirt with delicate dark-red inner lining.
- Long translucent black sleeves or lace cuffs.
- Black choker with a small ruby gemstone.
- Small black rose accessory in her hair.
- Minimal silver jewelry.

The outfit should feel sophisticated and stylish rather than costume-like.

### Alternative Outfit: Casual Date

- Black fitted sweater.
- Burgundy pleated skirt.
- Sheer black tights.
- Small silver earrings.
- Same choker and rose hair accessory.

### Alternative Outfit: Night Café

- Elegant dark burgundy blouse.
- Black blazer draped over her shoulders.
- Minimal silver necklace.
- Long black hair resting naturally over the shoulders.

**Consistency rule:** Her hair, eye color, choker, and rose accessory should remain recognizable across every outfit.

## 9. Character Consistency Rules

When generating new images, preserve these elements:

1. **Hair:** Long midnight-black hair with subtle violet highlights.
2. **Eyes:** Ruby-red irises with crimson gradients.
3. **Accessory:** Dark crimson rose on the left side of her head.
4. **Choker:** Black choker with a small ruby gemstone.
5. **Face:** Elegant adult anime facial proportions.
6. **Expression:** Confident and expressive, never permanently emotionless.
7. **Palette:** Primarily black, burgundy, crimson, and deep violet.
8. **Style:** Premium anime illustration with polished shading and clean line art.

### Avoid These Inconsistencies

- Changing ruby-red eyes to purple or blue.
- Making her hair short or bright-colored.
- Removing both the choker and rose accessory.
- Making her look like a child.
- Turning the gothic design into heavy armor or an elaborate fantasy costume.
- Making every expression seductive; she also needs ordinary, sincere emotions.

## 10. Recommended Generation Workflow

### Step 1 — Establish the Base Design

Generate a neutral bust portrait using the master prompt.

Choose an image with:
- Clear facial features.
- Both eyes visible.
- Recognizable hair silhouette.
- Visible rose accessory and choker.
- Balanced black and burgundy tones.

### Step 2 — Create a Reference Sheet

Generate a character sheet with:
- Front-facing portrait.
- Three-quarter view.
- Side profile.
- Full-body standing pose.
- Expression variations.

Keep the same outfit and hairstyle throughout the sheet.

### Step 3 — Generate Emotion Variants

Use the expression library to generate:
- Happy.
- Flirty.
- Jealous.
- Embarrassed.
- Quietly affectionate.
- Playfully annoyed.
- Mysterious.

### Step 4 — Generate Telegram Bot Assets

Recommended assets:

| Asset | Suggested Ratio | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Main avatar | 1:1 | Telegram bot profile |
| Chat portrait | 3:4 | Character display |
| Welcome image | 16:9 | Bot introduction |
| Expression icons | 1:1 | Chat emotion indicators |
| Full-body reference | 2:3 | Future character illustrations |

### Step 5 — Keep a Master Reference

Store the selected base portrait and use it as an image reference whenever the image-generation tool supports reference images or character consistency.

A prompt alone cannot guarantee perfect identity consistency. Reusing a reference image, fixed seed where supported, and the same model/settings will generally produce more consistent results.

## 12. Final Character Direction

Akira should feel like the girlfriend who teases first, laughs when her partner gets flustered, and then unexpectedly shows a softer side.

Her visual identity is built around **midnight-black hair, ruby-red eyes, a dark rose, and elegant gothic fashion**.

The key is contrast: mysterious but warm, confident but affectionate, flirtatious but capable of genuine emotional intimacy.

She should feel like a complete character—not just a collection of seductive expressions.
