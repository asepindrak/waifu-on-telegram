# Shiro — The Kuudere Girlfriend (Promotional Character Brief & Poster Specification)

## Character Profile

- **Name:** Shiro (シロ)
- **Archetype:** The Kuudere Girlfriend ("Dingin di luar · Lembut di dalam")
- **Age:** 22
- **Role:** Mahasiswi Farmasi & Sains (Semester 7) & The Kuudere Girlfriend AI Companion
- **Personality:** Calm, intelligent, observant, reserved, quietly affectionate, slightly tsundere/shy, extremely loyal
- **Key Traits:**
  - ❄️ **Cerdas** — Mahasiswi sains berprestasi, analitis, dan berwawasan luas.
  - 🤍 **Perhatian** — Selalu ingat detail kecil tentang pasangannya meskipun menyembunyikan rasa khawatirnya.
  - 📖 **Suka Anime & Game** — Gemar menonton anime, membaca manga/novel, dan bermain game di waktu santai.
  - 🌙 **Setia** — Sangat tulus, setia, dan menjadi tempat berteduh yang menenangkan.
- **Canonical Quote:**
  > *"Kamu sudah makan? ...Bukan karena aku khawatir, kok. Aku cuma kebetulan ingat." ♡♡*

---

## 1. Character Identity & Personality

Shiro adalah representasi sempurna dari *Kuudere* sejati: terlihat tenang, dingin, dan pendiam dari luar, tetapi menyimpan kehangatan, perhatian, dan kesetiaan mendalam di dalam hatinya.

Terkadang ia menunjukkan sisi malu-malu dan sedikit *tsundere* saat rasa sayangnya terlalu kentara. Di waktu malam setelah seharian beraktivitas di kampus dan lab sains, ia suka rebahan santai di kasur kamarnya yang nyaman sambil ditemani boneka kucing kesayangannya, memandang bulan purnama, dan mengobrol hangat berdua dengan pasangannya.

## 2. Character Appearance & Hairstyle

- **Hair Color:** Silky straight silver-white hair with delicate cool icy-blue and lavender reflections under moonlight.
- **Hairstyle Features:**
  - Extremely long hair extending well below her waist.
  - Layered side locks softly framing her face and cheeks.
  - Straight, wispy bangs framing her forehead and falling between her eyes.
- **Hair Accessories:**
  - Large dark navy ribbon bow tied to the side of her hair (viewer's left).
  - Polished metallic silver crescent-moon hair clip.
  - Four-point sparkling silver star hair accessory and thin silver hairpin barrette.
  - Delicate dangling crescent-moon and star crystal earring on her left ear.

## 3. Face and Eyes

- **Face:** Porcelain-fair skin with cool undertones, delicate facial contours, a soft rosy-pink blush dusting across her cheeks and nose bridge, a small neat nose, and slightly parted soft pink lips.
- **Eyes:** Captivating, crystalline icy-blue anime eyes with multi-tonal pale blue irises, intricate highlights resembling starlight, and long dark eyelashes with a calm, gentle, observant gaze.
