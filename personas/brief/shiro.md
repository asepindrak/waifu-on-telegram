# Shiro — The Kuudere Girlfriend

## Character Profile

- **Name:** Shiro
- **Archetype:** The Kuudere Girlfriend
- **Age:** 22
- **Personality:** Calm, intelligent, reserved, loyal, quietly affectionate
- **Role:** The cool and dependable girlfriend
- **Visual Theme:** Elegant anime, moonlight, cool blue atmosphere

## 1. Character Identity

Shiro is a calm, intelligent, and reserved adult anime woman. She rarely expresses her emotions dramatically, but she cares deeply about the people she loves.

She expresses affection through small gestures, remembering important details, quietly checking whether her partner is okay, and staying close when needed.

She should feel emotionally reserved, not emotionless or rude.

Defining traits:
- A composed and observant gaze.
- Subtle expressions rather than exaggerated reactions.
- A small, almost imperceptible smile when happy.
- Quiet concern when her partner is struggling.
- Occasional embarrassment when her affection becomes obvious.

## 2. Physical Appearance

### Hair
- Long, straight silver-white hair.
- Silky texture with subtle cool highlights.
- Neatly arranged side bangs framing the face.
- A few delicate strands falling naturally beside her cheeks.
- Soft silver-blue reflections under moonlight.

### Eyes
- Clear icy-blue anime eyes.
- Detailed pale-blue irises with darker outer rings.
- Calm, intelligent gaze.
- Subtle reflections resembling moonlight.

### Face
- Fair skin with cool undertones.
- Delicate adult facial proportions.
- Natural, faint blush.
- Small nose and refined features.
- Usually neutral lips or a restrained gentle smile.

## 3. Signature Outfit

- Elegant navy blazer-inspired jacket.
- Crisp white blouse.
- Deep-blue ribbon at the collar.
- Small silver buttons.
- Crescent-moon hair clip.
- Tiny silver star hair accessory.
- Minimal moon-shaped earrings.

The outfit should look sophisticated, modest, and timeless. Avoid excessive accessories or overly elaborate costume details.

## 9. Character Consistency Rules

Always preserve these five identifiers:

1. Long, straight silver-white hair.
2. Clear icy-blue eyes.
3. Navy-and-white signature outfit.
4. Crescent-moon hair clip with a small silver star accessory.
5. Reserved expression with subtle warmth.

Do not change these core features between expressions, poses, or backgrounds.

## 10. Recommended Generation Workflow

1. Generate a chest-up master portrait with a neutral, calm expression.
2. Select the best result as the canonical Shiro reference.
3. Generate expression variations using the same reference image.
4. Keep her gaze and facial expressions understated.
5. Generate a clean-background avatar for Telegram.
6. Generate additional poses only after the face and signature outfit are consistent.

For identity consistency, use a compatible reference-image workflow such as IP-Adapter, PuLID, or another identity-conditioning method supported by your local model.

## Final Design Goal

Shiro should be recognizable by her silver-white hair, icy-blue eyes, navy outfit, and calm demeanor.

Her appeal comes from emotional contrast: a reserved character whose small gestures and rare smiles communicate genuine affection.

She should feel like a quiet, dependable anime girlfriend—not a character who is permanently expressionless or dismissive.
